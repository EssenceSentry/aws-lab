Your progress, your choice.

Waypoint is a personal study app for the AWS SAP-C02 exam. You can use it without an account. Updated September 13, 2026.

Saved on your device

Your answers, study statistics, bookmarks, session history, unfinished session, and appearance preference are saved in your browser. Questions, the guide, and downloaded diagrams are cached for offline use. Waypoint has no advertising or analytics.

Optional Google Drive sync

When you connect Google Drive, Google provides your account identifier and email so Waypoint can show which account you selected and track the last successful sync. Your short-lived Google access token is kept in memory only and disappears when you close or reload the app.

Only a tap on the sync or backup controls reads or writes Drive. Waypoint uses the private application-data folder and cannot browse your regular Drive files. Backups include your study progress and unfinished session, a device category, a question-bank compatibility identifier, and the time saved. Appearance stays specific to each device. Data travels directly between your browser and Google over HTTPS; there is no Waypoint server holding your backups.

Recovery and deletion

Earlier Drive backups are retained so you can recover from a mistaken choice. Settings shows the ten most recent backups. A local copy is also kept before your last sync and can be exported as a JSON file.

Disconnect in Settings forgets the chosen account and access token on that device; it keeps your progress and Drive backups. To revoke access, remove Waypoint in your Google Account connections. To delete cloud backups, open Google Drive Settings → Manage apps → Waypoint → Options → Delete hidden app data. Clear this site’s browser storage to remove local progress, recovery copies, and sync metadata. Downloaded JSON files remain wherever you saved them.

Service providers

GitHub Pages serves the app. Google supplies sign-in and optional Drive storage and handles data under its own privacy policy. Opening sign-in loads Google’s identity library. External reference links and native sharing use the destinations you choose. Waypoint does not sell your data or use it to train AI models.

Waypoint’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
